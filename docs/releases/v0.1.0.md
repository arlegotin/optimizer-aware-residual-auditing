# OAR v0.1.0 — 2026-10-31

This source release packages the existing optimizer-aware residual auditing implementation and its stopped-study evidence. It adds publication metadata, citation and licensing; it introduces no runtime behavior, dependencies or new research.

## Reusable components

- Projected linear weight gradients with exact incoming adjoints and streaming microbatch accumulation.
- Transactional AdamW preview/commit, optimizer displacement alignment checks and explicit unsupported-layout rejection.
- Fresh residual probes and a durable confidence-spending journal with fail-closed numerical decisions.
- Synchronized native MPS component-cost accounting, a bounded synthetic oracle and hash-verified current/historical report generation.

## Recorded evidence and limits

On the measured Apple M3 Max native arm64 CPython 3.13.5 / PyTorch 2.15.0 MPS FP32 environment, the wide block's candidate reconstruction took 28.875 ms against paired native AdamW's 36.882 ms. The complete conditional zero-rejection attempt took 54.406 ms against its paired native 36.738 ms. Paired exploratory 95% saving intervals were 7.395–8.605 ms for the candidate and −19.052 to −15.941 ms for the complete attempt. Every complete attempt in all six measured configurations was slower. These are residual-block measurements under recorded resource pressure, not training throughput or time-to-quality results. Forced conditional commits price a path without authorizing online acceptance.

The bounded synthetic oracle contains 792 layer rows / 396 proposals from six condition-seed histories and two initialization/data seeds. All 144 past-only evaluation proposals reject at every declared exact-margin threshold; all lose development progress versus matched dense updates. Correlated proposals and duplicated early stages limit inference. A fresh same-seed repeat matched all 792 rows and 30 learning-curve observations exactly after an explicitly recorded comparison-schema correction; the first comparison remains failed. This establishes numerical reproducibility, not independent confirmation.

Measurements retain their original source identities: the synthetic study at `2e89e742131eb5eebaeb7a028d8e8f2e4fc50e07` and timing sources recorded in the [evidence index](../../analysis/evidence_index.json). Later validation maintenance, including `7628be7792030e3cfbb17dba82d3517642c866ca`, was tested but not remeasured. The accepted pre-publication implementation verification recorded 364 passing tests, including 9 actual-MPS tests with fallback disabled. Publication validation is separate maintenance evidence in WP09.

Normal online research remains stopped. NumericalPolicy is unresolved. No corpus, full trainer/controller, language-model evaluation, end-to-end training gain or protected confirmation exists. See the [findings](../../analysis/report.md) and [accepted final review](../../decisions/reports/final_review.md).
