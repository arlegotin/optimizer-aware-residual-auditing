# OAR v0.2.0

This research release adds fixed-budget importance and uniform weight-gradient
sampling, the byte-Transformer study, recovery checks and a dependency-free
public-result replay. Native forward context and raw input/bias gradients stay
exact. AdamW and global clipping act on the sampled weight gradients.

The M3 Max FP32 language study completed27 configurations at one tuning seed.
Best development NLL was1.2316 dense,1.3196 uniform,1.3445 importance. All15
sampled arms were target-censored and missed the endpoint-quality guard. Controlled
step savings did not establish faster learning. The original stopped OAR audit,
its negative costs and0/144 synthetic result remain unchanged.
