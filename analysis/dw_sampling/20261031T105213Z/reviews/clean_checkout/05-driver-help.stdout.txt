usage: run_dw_sampling.py [-h]
                          --stage {prepare,feasibility,correctness,cost,pilot,confirm,summarize}
                          --config CONFIG --out OUT [--dry-run]

Bounded, append-only dW sampling experiment. Importing never starts work. Only
A--D are implemented before their gates. Corpus/trainer work stays absent
while the recorded asset restriction is unresolved. No old oracle is invoked.

options:

  --stage {prepare,feasibility,correctness,cost,pilot,confirm,summarize}
  --config CONFIG
  --out OUT
  --dry-run             prepare inventory only; no tests, training, bodies or
                        device operations
